## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size close-up 62%, medium shot 20%, medium close-up 18%; subject is a face (shot size from face height)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 86%, handheld 14%; typical move pan / track
- Lighting: soft frontal light (flat light). Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), from above (ceiling light / street lamp / high source); lighting ratio about 1.2:1 (0.2 stops); soft light (wide lit-to-shadow ramp); colour temperature warm (about 3000–4000K); subject and background differ in colour temperature: mixed sources (e.g. warm lamp + cool window light / screen); background sharp
- Grade: natural (close to the original colours); normal saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall neutral
- Editing: 14.5 cuts per minute, 3.75s average live-action shot; 4 camera set-up(s) / location(s); 4 jump cut(s) and 0 punch-in(s) within the same set-up; inserts (screenshots / screen recordings / cards / stock) take 0%; 30% of cuts land in pauses of the speech
- Format: talking head (face as subject 62%, objects / hands as subject 38%, handheld 100%); image readers: Phone-filmed demo: a real screen walked through by hand, then a selfie explanation
