## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size medium shot 100%; subject is a face (shot size from face height)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 51%, fixed camera (tripod / mount / on the desk) 49%; typical move pan / track
- Lighting: soft frontal light (flat light). Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), from above (ceiling light / street lamp / high source); lighting ratio about 1.2:1 (0.3 stops); soft light (wide lit-to-shadow ramp); colour temperature warm (about 3000–4000K); subject and background differ in colour temperature: mixed sources (e.g. warm lamp + cool window light / screen); background slightly soft
- Grade: natural (close to the original colours); normal saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall neutral
- Editing: 26.3 cuts per minute, 2.14s average live-action shot; 3 camera set-up(s) / location(s); 1 jump cut(s) and 0 punch-in(s) within the same set-up; inserts (screenshots / screen recordings / cards / stock) take 26%; 56% of cuts land in pauses of the speech
- Format: talking head (face as subject 100%, objects / hands as subject 0%, handheld 51%); image readers: Scripted talking-head list video with motion-graphic and screenshot inserts
