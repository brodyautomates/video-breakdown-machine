## Camera, lighting and editing

- Framing: shot size medium shot 98%, medium close-up 2%; subject is a face (shot size from face height)
- Camera: moving camera (gimbal / handheld pan) 94%, fixed camera (tripod / mount / on the desk) 6%; typical move push-in
- Lighting: night / low-light ambient, no dedicated fill. Key light frontal (from the camera side; both sides of the subject about equally bright), from above (ceiling light / street lamp / high source); lighting ratio about 1.2:1 (0.2 stops); soft light (almost no lit / shadow edge); colour temperature neutral (about 5000K); subject and background differ in colour temperature: mixed sources (e.g. warm lamp + cool window light / screen); background clearly blurred (shallow depth of field / portrait mode)
- Grade: natural (close to the original colours); high saturation, normal contrast, normal blacks, overall cool
- Editing: 29.8 cuts per minute, 1.93s average live-action shot; 1 camera set-up(s) / location(s); 3 jump cut(s) and 0 punch-in(s) within the same set-up; inserts (screenshots / screen recordings / cards / stock) take 48%; 67% of cuts land in pauses of the speech (cutting on breaths)
- Format: talking head (face as subject 100%, objects / hands as subject 0%, handheld 94%); image readers: Scripted talking-head explainer with workflow graphics and phone screen recordings
